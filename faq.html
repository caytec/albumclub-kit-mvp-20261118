AlbumClub Kit FAQ

Is this an album database?

No. The tool does not research albums or verify facts. Your supplied album title, artist, and tracklist are authoritative.

Can it work without an API key?

Yes. The default browser experience uses deterministic rules and does not make a network request. A Vercel deployment can optionally enable a server-side provider.

Does it reproduce lyrics?

No. Lyrics, audio, and automatically sourced artwork are outside the product scope.

Can I edit the result?

Yes. Questions and prompts are rendered as editable fields before copying or sharing.

How many tracks are supported?

The interface keeps the first fifteen non-empty tracks so the generated kit remains concise and usable during a real listening session.

Privacy notice

AlbumClub Kit is designed without accounts or permanent result storage. Form data is kept in your browser while you use the page. A share URL may encode the generated kit in the URL; do not share it if your content is private.

AI processing

If an administrator enables Gemini or Groq generation, submitted album details and optional reactions may be sent to the selected provider to generate prompts. The application does not intentionally store generated kits or complete notes, but hosting and provider systems may retain operational logs according to their policies.

Rules-only mode

When no API URL is configured in the browser, the kit is generated locally with deterministic rules. The default static deployment does not send generation requests to a remote API.

Logging

The MVP does not intentionally log complete notes or generated kits. Hosting providers may retain normal technical request logs for security and operations.

Copyright

Do not submit or request lyrics. The tool does not fetch audio or album artwork.

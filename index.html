Album listening-club questions FAQ

What makes a good album discussion question?

Open-ended questions about sequencing, contrast, mood, production impressions, and personal response give a group room to disagree without requiring trivia or unverified facts.

Can I use AlbumClub Kit without an AI key?

Yes. The browser and server both include a deterministic rules-based fallback, so the core kit remains usable when an AI provider is unavailable.

Does the tool display lyrics or album artwork?

No. The MVP uses only the album information and tracklist you provide and creates a typographic discussion card without lyrics, audio, or automatically sourced artwork.

Turn music listening into a better conversation

AlbumClub Kit helps a group move beyond “did you like it?” with prompts about order, contrast, favorite moments, surprise, and personal connection.

Made for real groups

Choose a casual, thoughtful, or deep-dive tone. Add a first impression or favorite track if you want the prompts to reflect your group’s starting point. You can edit every generated question before copying or sharing it.

Use it in different settings

Friends can use the poll to break a tie, teachers can use the agenda to structure reflection, and podcasters can use the questions as a starting outline. The tool stays focused on discussion rather than unsupported music trivia.

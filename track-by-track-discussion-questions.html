Track-by-track prompts

Give every track a place in the conversation

Enter one track per line and receive a concise prompt for each of the first fifteen tracks, plus broader questions about sequencing and the album as a whole.

Prompt examples

  • What detail first caught your attention here?
  • How does this track relate to the one before it?
  • What role does this song seem to play in the full listening experience?

Why track-by-track prompts help

A tracklist gives every participant a shared map of the session. Prompts can focus attention on transitions, contrasts, recurring reactions, and moments that might otherwise pass by unnoticed.

These are invitations to compare impressions, not claims about what an artist intended.
